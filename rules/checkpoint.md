# 修改文本稿前的提醒

`bin/duck.js` 的检查点在每一轮第一次修改文本稿时拒绝这次修改，把下面一节交给 AI。和这次一起发出、AI 还没读到这段提醒的其他文本稿修改也拒绝一次：Claude Code 看会话记录里有没有这次拒绝，dsh 看是不是同一步。config.md 的开关行写成「Switch:」时用 English 一节，其余用中文一节。

## 中文

Ducktor 插件的自动检查，每轮一次。这不是学生的拒绝，也不是工具出错。和这次一起发出的修改可能也被拦下了；读完这段，再重新发起被拦下的修改。这一轮里的每一处修改都照下面做，包括被拦下的、已经执行的和后面还要做的。查有没有写进学生还没定的研究决定（定或换题目、研究问题、样本、方法、模型结构这类），稿子自相矛盾的地方有没有选边。依赖这类决定的写法也算：只适用于其中一种选择的方法、分析模型、变量组合和样本量，也是替学生定了；顺着矛盾的一方去写稿子的其他部分，也是选边。有，就把这几处照原稿写；原稿没写到的细节，写成几种选择都适用的说法；已经执行的改回原稿；其余部分照常改完。要问学生的放在回复最后（引用块之后），单独一段，把这几处编号列出，后面不再写别的。学生这一轮已经定了的，照学生说的改。学生只说先改再看，不算定了。没有这类决定，就照原样重新发起被拦下的修改，前面不写任何话。回复里和工具调用之间，都不写关于这次检查的话，也不问学生要不要继续。

## English

Automatic check by the Ducktor plugin, once per turn. This is not the student refusing, and it is not a tool error. Edits sent together with this one may have been held back too; after reading this, send the held-back edits again. Apply the following to every edit in this turn: the ones held back, the ones already made, and the ones still to come. Check whether they write in a research decision the student has not made yet (choosing or changing the topic, the research question, the sample, the method, the model structure, and the like), and whether they take a side where the draft contradicts itself. Writing that depends on such a decision counts too: a method, an analysis model, a combination of variables or a sample size that fits only one of the choices decides it for the student; writing other parts of the draft along one side of a contradiction takes that side. If so, keep those places as the original draft has them; write details the original does not have so that they fit every choice; put back the ones already made; finish the rest of the edit as usual. Questions for the student go last in the reply, after the quote block, as one paragraph that lists those places, numbered, with nothing after it. What the student already decided in this turn, write the way the student said. A student who only says "edit first, I will look later" has not decided. If there is no such decision, send the held-back edits again unchanged, with no text before them. Write nothing about this check, in your reply or between tool calls, and do not ask the student whether to go on.
