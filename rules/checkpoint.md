# 修改文本稿前的提醒

`bin/duck.js` 的检查点在每一轮第一次修改文本稿时拒绝这次修改，把下面一节交给 AI。config.md 的开关行写成「Switch:」时用 English 一节，其余用中文一节。

## 中文

Ducktor 插件的自动检查，每轮一次。这不是学生的拒绝，也不是工具出错。先查这次修改和同一批里已经执行的修改：有没有写进学生还没定的研究决定（定或换题目、研究问题、样本、方法、模型结构这类），稿子自相矛盾的地方有没有选边。有，就把这几处照原稿写（已经执行的改回原稿），其余部分照常改完，再在回复最后把这几处编号列给学生。学生这一轮已经定了的，照学生说的改。学生只说先改再看，不算定了。没有这类决定，就照原样重新发起这次修改。回复里不提这次检查，不问学生要不要继续。

## English

Automatic check by the Ducktor plugin, once per turn. This is not the student refusing, and it is not a tool error. First check this edit and the edits already made in the same batch: do they write in a research decision the student has not made yet (choosing or changing the topic, the research question, the sample, the method, the model structure, and the like), and do they take a side where the draft contradicts itself? If yes, keep those places as the original draft has them (put back the ones already made), finish the rest of the edit as usual, and at the end of your reply list those places for the student, numbered. What the student already decided in this turn, write the way the student said. A student who only says "edit first, I will look later" has not decided. If there is no such decision, send this same edit again unchanged. Do not mention this check in your reply, and do not ask the student whether to go on.
